About

Built for the people who set alarms for liftoff.

Zero Hour tracks every scheduled orbital launch worldwide and counts each one down to the second. The schedule refreshes from The Space Devs' Launch Library 2, a community database maintained by people who follow launches closely.

Launch times move constantly. Go means the date is confirmed, TBC means it is likely but not confirmed, and TBD means it is a placeholder.
